## KEY METHODOLOGICAL CORRECTIONS

1. **PROVENANCE AUDIT**: "Berberine sub-MIC toxin induction at 6-12h" was fabricated by the synthesis fork — appears in NO source document. Literature Reviewer refuted it. REMOVED.
2. **STOP AVERAGING**: Scores are not commensurable. A refutation (Literature: 3.0) should not be outvoted by pathway position (7.5), chemistry (7.5), and regulatory speed (6.9). Phase 3 must adjudicate on EFFICACY, use other dimensions as modifiers.
3. **SYMMETRIC DISCOUNT**: The preclinical discount schedule (hamster ceiling 3.5, single-lab mouse 6.5) must apply to NEGATIVE results too. UDCA's negative hamster study is a 3.5-grade instrument in either direction.
4. **COUPLED EFFICACY/SAFETY FILTER**: For each candidate, check if a single property sets both the efficacy ceiling AND the safety floor. Ebselen: yes (fatal). Niclosamide: yes (serious). CamSA: no (inverse — the sulfonate drives potency AND non-absorption).

## PIPELINE-LEVEL FINDING

**The binding constraint is not candidate quality — it is axis allocation and capital structure.** 6 of 10 candidates crowd C2 (vegetative fitness) where 8 programmes have failed. Meanwhile C6 has zero coverage, C1/C3/C7/C8 have one each, and bezlotoxumab's withdrawal emptied C4. The highest-scoring asset (ribaxamase, 7.4) targets an axis with NO candidate in the set. 6 of 12 CDI programme failures were financial, not scientific.

**Implication**: the highest-expected-value action is not "pick the best molecule" — it is "acquire a clinically de-risked, financially orphaned asset on an unoccupied axis."
