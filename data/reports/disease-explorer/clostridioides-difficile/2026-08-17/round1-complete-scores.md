## KEY AGREEMENTS (≥4 agents agree)

1. **UDCA is the strongest non-benchmark development candidate** — ranked top non-benchmark by Chemist, Clinical, Ethnobotany; tight range (6.0–7.5). Upgraded to mean 7.1 with Ethnobotany's 7.5.

2. **Berberine upgraded with Ethnobotany endorsement** — mean rises from 6.4 to 6.7. Ethnobotany (8.0) and ADMET (8.0) agree it has the best luminal profile among non-pharmaceutical candidates. Chemist (5.0) remains the dissenter on fecal-binding and redox grounds.

3. **Fidaxomicin is the undisputed benchmark** — mean 8.9, highest or near-highest by every agent.

4. **Conessine should be deprioritized** — bottom 2 by 4/6 agents. Chemist largely falsified CspC scaffold hypothesis. Ethnobotany (5.5) is the most favorable but labels it "screen computationally, do not develop as-is."

5. **Non-absorption is the dominant positive predictor** — flagged independently by all 6 agents.

6. **Narrow-spectrum antibacterial is a burned value proposition** — all agents flag ibezapolstat's strategic problem.

## KEY CONFLICTS (agents disagree by ≥3 points)

1. **Ebselen: Target (8.5) vs ADMET (4.0)** — 4.5-point spread. Central question: is the Se warhead viable in the reducing colon?

2. **Vancomycin: ADMET (9.0) vs Disease (4.0)** — 5.0-point spread. Both correct: perfect PK, terrible disease biology. CDI's central paradox.

3. **Niclosamide: Disease (7.5) vs ADMET (3.0)** — 4.5-point spread. Nitro reduction in anaerobic environment may destroy pharmacophore.

4. **Berberine: Ethno (8.0) + ADMET (8.0) vs Chemist (5.0)** — 3.0-point spread. Quaternary cation = luminal confinement asset vs fecal-binding liability. Fecal-matrix MIC is decisive.

## ETHNOBOTANY-SPECIFIC FINDINGS

### Strongest contribution: Cross-cutting observation on "failed bioavailability as CDI screening filter"
The Ethnobotany agent's most impactful finding: compounds abandoned for poor bioavailability in other indications should be *positively* screened for CDI, where non-absorption is the design specification. This inverts 30 years of natural product triage.

### New candidates surfaced
- **Oral hyperimmune bovine colostrum/IgY** (6.0) — only candidate satisfying all five §A.1 screening questions; best selectivity in the set
- **Cyperus rotundus (Musta)** — recommended addition to Natural Product Scout list; oleanolic acid → TGR5/FXR link
- **Gedunin/celastrol → Hsp90 → TcdB translocation blockade** — novel anti-virulence hypothesis generated from project data

### Key questions for Phase 2
- To Safety Pharmacologist: Does berberine's luminal P-gp inhibition create DDI risk even without systemic absorption?
- To Chemist/ADMET: Does berberine's quaternary ammonium charge cause fecal binding to dominate over luminal confinement benefit?
- To Literature Reviewer: Verify powered CDI trial data for oral colostrum/IgY; verify colonic UDCA concentrations in dysbiotic patients

## GAPS FOR PHASE 2

1. **No candidate RESTORES colonization resistance** (Phase 0/Module C RESTORE) — biggest strategic hole
2. **Phase 1 (germination) has no credible small-molecule candidate** after conessine downgrade; UDCA is the only option
3. **Phase 3 (toxin gene regulation) is empty** — no TcdR/CodY/CcpA candidates
4. **Phase 4 (epithelial repair) is unaddressed**
5. **Ebselen colonic viability** — central unresolved question
6. **Berberine fecal binding** — experimental data needed
7. **UDCA colonic delivery** — pharmacokinetic question (parent vs LCA downstream)
8. **Commensal spectrum data** — NO candidate has measured MICs against the protective commensal guild
